By the time an idea reaches your feed, the edge has already moved. Three frustrations this letter exists to resolve.

— The noise

Most retail trading content is content — produced to be consumed, not acted on. By the time a "pick" reaches you, it has been seen by ten thousand other accounts and the easy part of the move is gone.

— The volume

You do not need another watchlist. You need fewer, better ideas — with the research already done, the levels already drawn, and the exit defined before you are in the trade.

— The conviction

You want conviction you can act on — not forty-seven "picks of the week," not a Discord ping at half past nine. A thesis you can read in five minutes and stand behind.

Inventory has cleaned up over two consecutive quarters and gross margin is reverting toward its five-year mean — while the tape still prices the name as if the destock cycle were ongoing. Sentiment is washed out; insiders bought the last drawdown.
