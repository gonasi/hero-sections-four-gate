> 02 · the problem

Most trading content is built to be scrolled, not traded.

By the time an idea reaches your feed, the edge has already moved. Here's the gap Four Gate Alpha exists to close.

the noise problem

Most retail trading content is content — produced to be consumed, not acted on. By the time a "pick" reaches you, it has been seen by ten thousand other accounts and the easy part of the move is gone.

the volume problem

You don't need another watchlist. You need fewer, better ideas — with the research already done, the levels already drawn, and the exit defined before you're in the trade.

the conviction problem

You want conviction you can actually act on — not forty-seven "picks of the week," not a Discord ping at 9:31. A thesis you can read in five minutes and stand behind.
