Risk Disclosure

Four Gate Alpha provides general investment research and commentary. Nothing on this website is personalised investment advice. Trading involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

Trading is risky

The value of securities can go down as well as up. You can lose some or all of the money you put at risk, and in leveraged instruments you can lose more than your initial capital. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

About the trade ideas we publish

Our ideas are opinions, formed from publicly available information, about how a given setup might play out. They can be — and sometimes will be — wrong. We may hold, or later take, positions in the securities we write about. We are not obligated to update an idea after it's published, and we may close or change our own view without notice.

Where we show results of past ideas, those figures are hypothetical performance of published ideas, assuming entry and exit at the levels we published. They do not reflect trading costs, slippage, taxes, or the timing and sizing decisions of any individual subscriber. Actual member results will differ — often materially.

You are responsible for evaluating any idea against your own objectives, risk tolerance, time horizon, and circumstances — ideally with a licensed professional who knows your situation. Using this site or our research is not a substitute for that.
