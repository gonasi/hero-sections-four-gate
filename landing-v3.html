— Thesis

Inventory has cleaned up over two consecutive quarters and gross margin is reverting toward its five-year mean — while the tape still prices the name as if the destock cycle were ongoing. Sentiment is washed out; insiders bought the last drawdown.

The setup is a re-test of the prior earnings-day low on declining volume. We're a buyer on the test, not the breakout — entry staggered across the band, stop under the structural low.

What invalidates it: a weekly close below 207, or a guide-down that resets the gross-margin story. If either prints, the idea is dead — no averaging down.
